--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Patent / Royalty Notice (MPEG-LA AVC pool)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

H.264 / AVC is a patented technology pooled by MPEG-LA. Cisco's pre-paid royalty
coverage applies only when the OpenH264 binary is downloaded by the end user
directly from `http://ciscobinary.openh264.org` at install or first run.

This product BUNDLES the OpenH264 native library inside the installer package.
Per Cisco's openh264.org FAQ, bundled binaries are NOT covered by Cisco's
royalty pre-payment.

Distribution scope (current):
  * Below 100,000 units / year of distributed product → no royalty fee under
    MPEG-LA AVC pool's free tier (encoder & decoder).
  * Distribution counted per ClassroomCtrl install (Teacher + Student combined
    is one product).

Action items if shipping volume exceeds the free tier:
  1. Purchase MPEG-LA AVC license directly, OR
  2. Migrate to runtime download of OpenH264 from Cisco-hosted URL
     (Firefox model — see https://www.openh264.org/faq.html for details).
  3. Consult legal counsel before scaling production distribution beyond
     pilot programs.
