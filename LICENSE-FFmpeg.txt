--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Patent / codec notice
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Recording produces MP4 files containing H.264 video and AAC audio. Both codecs
are subject to patent licensing (MPEG-LA AVC pool + MPEG-LA AAC patent pool).
The FFmpeg distribution does NOT include patent licenses for these codecs —
distributors are responsible for their own compliance.

ClassroomCtrl operates under MPEG-LA's first-100,000-units/year free tier for
both AVC encoder/decoder and AAC. See LICENSE-OpenH264.txt for the H.264
discussion; AAC distribution is also covered under the same volume threshold.

Action items if shipping volume exceeds the free tier:
  1. Purchase MPEG-LA AVC + AAC licenses directly, OR
  2. Replace bundled FFmpeg with a build that disables AAC and falls back to
     a royalty-free audio codec (e.g. Opus in MP4 — limited player support).
  3. Consult legal counsel before scaling production distribution beyond
     pilot programs.
